The Bot values your privacy. We want to assure you that we do not store any personal data, including your username, avatar, messages, or token. 

### Data Collection & Usage
* **User Data:** The only personal information we retain is your user ID, which is necessary for associating your account with moderation actions such as reports and bans.
* **Server Data:** To power community features, the bot may collect and publicly display server information (such as your server's name and server ID) in specific public features, including public leaderboards or the top server lists. 

### Data Removal & Opt-Out
* If you do not wish your server to appear in these public features, or if you ever wish to completely delete your data, you can request this at any time through our support server. 

We are committed to maintaining the security and confidentiality of your information and ensuring that it is used only for its intended purposes.
