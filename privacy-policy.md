# Privacy Policy ZenithiumX

**Last Updated:** July 8, 2026

The **ZenithiumX Development Team** ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users ("you", "your"). This Privacy Policy explains what data **ZenithiumX** ("the Bot") collects, how it is processed, and your rights regarding this information. 

By adding the Bot to your server or interacting with it, you explicitly agree to the data practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Data Collection & Core Usage

To function correctly, provide automation features, and ensure infrastructure security, ZenithiumX processes and stores specific data. We only collect the bare minimum required for operation.

### A. User Data
* **Stored Data:** We store **Discord User IDs** ($\text{e.g., } 123456789012345678$). 
* **Purpose:** This unique identifier is strictly used to link your account to internal Bot systems, such as user profiles, economy stats, custom configurations, and moderation records (such as global bans, warnings, or reports).
* **Temporary Processing:** The Bot processes your username, discriminator, and avatar inside Discord's gateway to render embeds or profiles, but **we do not store** your passwords, tokens, messages, or personal media files on our external databases.

### B. Server (Guild) Data
* **Stored Data:** **Server IDs**, **Server Names**, and **Server Icon Hashes**.
* **Purpose:** This data is essential for saving server-specific configurations, moderation logs, custom roles, and prefixes.
* **Public Display:** To power community features, server information (such as your server's name, member count, and icon) may be publicly displayed in specific features, including public leaderboards, top server lists, or our official dashboard.

---

## 2. Data Security & Protection

* **Storage & Access:** All stored data is hosted on secured databases with strictly restricted access. Only authorized core developers of the ZenithiumX Team have access to the database layer.
* **Third-Party Sharing:** We **never** sell, rent, or trade your server or user data to third parties. Data is never shared externally, except if explicitly required by law enforcement or to comply with Discord's official Trust & Safety investigations.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Opt-Out Rights

We believe you should have full control over your data. You have the right to access, modify, or delete your information at any time.

* **Automatic Deletion:** When ZenithiumX is kicked or banned from a Discord server, the server's configuration data may be retained for a limited grace period (to preserve settings in case of accidental removal) before being permanently deleted from our active databases.
* **Public Feature Opt-Out:** If you do not want your server to appear on public leaderboards or server lists, you can disable this option by opening a request on our support server.
* **Manual Data Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** You can request the complete and permanent deletion of all stored data related to your User ID or Server ID at any time. To do so, please join our **Official Support Server** and open a data deletion ticket.

---

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our features, Discord's developer policy, or applicable laws. Any modifications will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued interaction with the Bot after these changes implies acceptance of the updated policy.
